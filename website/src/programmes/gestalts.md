# Cognitive Bias and Gestalts

<p class="subtitle">Seeing Beyond Legacy Mental Models in a Changing Environment</p>

### Four key outcomes

1. **How Gestalts Mediate Reality:** Exploring how personal and shared organizational Gestalts filter data, shape blind spots, and reinforce cognitive biases.
2. **Subjects vs. Objects:** Moving from detached, mechanical analysis (Erklären) to understanding how people actually experience and interpret their environment (Verstehen).
3. **When Past Maps Fail:** Identifying situations where Gestalts that produced success in the past have become rigid or inefficient in handling a changed market.
4. **Reorganizing for New Insight:** Using dialogue and alternative perspectives to deconstruct outdated assumptions and foster adaptive self-organization.

<p class="meta"><strong>Ideal for:</strong> Leadership teams, strategy groups, and functional heads navigating market disruption or organizational change.</p>
<p class="meta"><strong>Format:</strong> 1- to 2-day interactive workshop or executive offsite (online or offline).</p>
