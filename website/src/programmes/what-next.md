# What Next

<p class="subtitle">Clarity, Choice, and Action When the Stakes Are High</p>

### Four key outcomes

1. **Clearing Space to Pay Attention:** Setting apart dedicated, distraction-free mind space so complex decisions are not squeezed into busy margins.
2. **Widening the Pool Before Looking at Evidence:** Holding back premature data-anchoring to bring wild, out-of-the-box possibilities into the mix alongside conventional options.
3. **Dialectical Stress-Testing:** Constructing and defending the strongest possible case for every position against rigorous opposition.
4. **Committing Beyond Outcome Anxiety:** Confronting the real cost of delay and translating the choice into concrete 'What Next' implementation steps, supported where needed by external research into customer, employee, and stakeholder perspectives.

<p class="meta"><strong>Ideal for:</strong> Founders, CXOs, boards, and senior leadership teams facing high-stakes strategic choices.</p>
<p class="meta"><strong>Format:</strong> Confidential 1-on-1 executive sessions, team decision labs, or a 2-day leadership retreat (online or offline).</p>
