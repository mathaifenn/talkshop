# Y Work

<p class="subtitle">Meaning-Making and Human Flourishing Beyond the Economic Contract</p>

### Four key outcomes

1. **Deconstructing the Economic Argument:** Examining the limits of purely transactional incentives and uncovering the deeper human reasons people commit their energy and creativity.
2. **Beyond Problem-Solving to Growth:** Shifting organizational focus from merely fixing deficits and burnout symptoms to asking what it means to flourish and grow through everyday work.
3. **Questioning Cultural Scripts:** Deconstructing inherited assumptions around success, status, normality, and fulfilment.
4. **Meaning as an Organizational Compass:** Grounding engagement in lived human understanding (Verstehen) rather than quick-fix motivational hacks.

<p class="meta"><strong>Ideal for:</strong> Senior and mid-level leaders, high-potential talent, and teams navigating career transitions or engagement stagnation.</p>
<p class="meta"><strong>Format:</strong> 1- to 2-day reflective workshop or 4-week live online cohort journey.</p>
