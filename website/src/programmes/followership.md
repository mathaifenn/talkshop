# Followership

<p class="subtitle">Ego, Discipleship, and the Inward Discipline of Alignment</p>

### Four key outcomes

1. **Ego and Resistance:** Examining how status anxiety, turf protection, and ego-rigidity block alignment, and learning to flow like water according to the lie of the land.
2. **Discipline of Self-Change:** Practising Nishkama Karma (total commitment to effort without psychological dependence on outcomes) alongside Yama and Niyama.
3. **Intelligent Followership:** Interpreting strategic intent, staying oriented through uncertainty like following a pillar of cloud by day and fire by night, while questioning assumptions.
4. **Iterative Momentum:** Replacing perfectionism paralysis with rapid, ego-free experimentation and learning from setbacks.

<p class="meta"><strong>Ideal for:</strong> Mid-level managers, functional heads, and cross-functional team leads translating strategy into execution.</p>
<p class="meta"><strong>Format:</strong> 1- to 2-day experiential workshop or 5-week live online cohort journey.</p>
