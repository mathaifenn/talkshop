# H Factor

<p class="subtitle">Human Connection and Regard in the Theatre of Work</p>

### Four key outcomes

1. **From I–It to I–Thou:** Moving beyond relating to people as roles, designations, or metrics to recognizing them as independent, meaning-making subjects.
2. **The Gaze and 'Othering':** Becoming aware of how the way we look at, evaluate, or stereotype others across hierarchies and departments creates defensiveness or psychological safety.
3. **Seeing, Attending, and Listening:** Practising deep, undivided listening, rooted in Carl Rogers’ unconditional regard, rather than listening merely to debate or resolve a ticket.
4. **Experiential Connection Labs:** Using structured pair exercises, allowing oneself to be seen, maintaining natural presence, and reflective dialogue to restore authentic human warmth.

<p class="meta"><strong>Ideal for:</strong> Service-sector teams, client-facing professionals, B2B account managers, and cross-functional leaders.</p>
<p class="meta"><strong>Format:</strong> 1- to 2-day experiential encounter lab or 4-week live online practice cohort.</p>
