# AI, HI and NI

<p class="subtitle">Understanding Intelligence and the Irreplaceable Human Role</p>

### Four key outcomes

1. **Demystifying Intelligence:** Clarifying how computational pattern-matching differs from lived human consciousness and moral judgment.
2. **Cognitive Triage:** Allocating work across automatic intuition (System 1), slow deliberate human reasoning (System 2), and delegation to people or AI (System 3), anchored by the rule that delegation is never abdication.
3. **Thinking WITH AI:** Learning to ride the horse without losing the reins, using AI as an intellectual sparring partner to cross-examine arguments rather than letting it think for us.
4. **The Four Movements:** Cultivating Shoshin (Beginner’s Mind), Wu Wei (navigating ambiguity without anxiety), Kriti (creative synthesis), and Vimarsha (reflexive self-awareness).

<p class="meta"><strong>Ideal for:</strong> Senior leaders, functional heads, and knowledge or operations teams integrating AI into daily workflows.</p>
<p class="meta"><strong>Format:</strong> 1- to 2-day interactive lab or 4-week live online capability sprint.</p>
